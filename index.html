                    Wheel & controller

                    Works with USB steering wheels and pedals (Logitech, Thrustmaster, Fanatec, Moza, …) and gamepads (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro). Browsers can't drive a wheel's force-feedback motor; gamepads get rumble in Chrome and Edge.
